FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000):
Can you find the way out? Tap the arrows in the right order and clear the whole board!

Arrow Puzzle is a simple but addictive logic puzzle game. Every arrow slides in the direction it points – but only if its path is clear. Pick the wrong one and it gets blocked. Think ahead, plan your moves, and escape every arrow to win the level.

Easy to learn, hard to master – the perfect brain game for a quick break or a long relaxing session.

🏹 HOW TO PLAY
• Tap an arrow to send it flying in its direction
• An arrow can only escape if nothing is blocking its path
• Clear every arrow from the board to complete the level
• Avoid mistakes – you only have 3 hearts per level
• Earn up to 3 stars for a perfect run

🧠 500 HAND-CRAFTED LEVELS
